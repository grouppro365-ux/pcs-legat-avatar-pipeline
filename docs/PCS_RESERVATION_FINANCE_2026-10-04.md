# PCS reservation financial projection and balance

The canonical Supabase trigger previously marked a reservation fully paid for any linked paid entry, including expenses and security deposits. It also ignored partial amounts, refunds, amount/currency edits and deletes, and changed contact status and last_contact_at. The replacement projects payment_status from paid rent/service income in the reservation currency, less refunds linked to their paid original income. Booking advances count when actually linked to this canonical reservation; security_deposit/refundable_deposit income and entry_type deposit remain separate. Expenses and partner payouts never pay rent. Other currencies are displayed separately without invented FX conversion.

States are unpaid, partial, paid and refunded. Full payment requires a positive known total; missing/zero totals cannot produce paid. Refund originals must match reservation, effective contact and currency; paid refunds cannot exceed the original paid amount. Checks apply to linked canonical reservations independently of NAVI attribution. A paid linked entry cannot be deleted directly. Planned rows may be deleted. This is not an immutable accounting ledger: correcting/cancelling entries remains possible under existing server flows and constraints. Unlinked finance entries remain outside the new reservation guard; NAVI commission/revenue logic was preserved. Refunds against entry_type deposit are not implemented; security income refunds use their original income entry. No new financial mutation UI, bank confirmation, actual refund transfer or FX workflow was enabled.

BEFORE mutation triggers lock both affected reservations in UUID order. VOLATILE AFTER readers recalculate old and new reservations, and amount/status/type/kind/currency/reference edits are included. Price/currency changes also recalculate. PostgreSQL can abort overlapping multi-row mutations on deadlock; no automatic unsafe retry was added. The actual concurrent multi-session race remains unverified. Payment state changes and their balance are audited atomically. Manual CRM/deal stage/next action/last-contact and reservation service status are preserved. No historical mass backfill was performed: existing independent status is left until an affected ledger/terms mutation; the detail explicitly flags disagreement with the ledger calculation.

The authenticated manager finance-balance GET uses a service-only SECURITY INVOKER RPC. Narrow response fields omit receipt paths, metadata and contact details. The existing ledger detail shows total, receipts, refunds, net payment, remaining/overpayment, security amounts and other currencies. Late responses cannot overwrite a newer detail or a different page. Ledger status options now match the actual planned/due/paid/cancelled constraint, removing impossible pending/rejected filters. Decimal values remain strings; canonical money columns are numeric(12,2).
